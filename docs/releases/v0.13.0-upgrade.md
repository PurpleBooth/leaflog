# Upgrading to v0.13.0

Back up `/data` before you upgrade — the app directory that holds the
database and the photo originals. Home Assistant takes an automatic
backup before an app update, but a copy you made yourself is the safer
record.

This release replaces EC/pH feed plans with product-dose feeding
schedules, and the upgrade migration deletes the old template data
outright: the drop is one-way.

## What the upgrade deletes

Migration V19 hard-drops:

- the three builtin feed templates ("Generic soil feed" and friends)
  and their EC target rows;
- every fitted feed-plan day the app had generated from them — the
  µS/cm targets are gone, unconverted.

Nothing else is touched: grows, plants, log entries, photos, sensor
bindings and settings survive unchanged. No EC→dose conversion runs —
a converted guess would read as authoritative while being fiction.
The library ships empty and your charts start from your producer's
numbers, not from a machine guess.

## What you do after the upgrade

The **Schedules** page (new in the navigation) starts empty. Create a
feeding schedule from your nutrient producer's chart: add the products
as rows, pick each product's unit, and enter the dose ranges for
Propagation, Veg, each flowering week and the water tail. Assign the
schedule on the grow's edit page; the grow overview then fits it to
that grow's actual timeline and shows a this-week card.

Grows that had an EC plan fitted show no feed section until you assign
a schedule — including finished grows, whose old plan days are deleted
by the migration.

## How fitting behaves

A schedule fits each grow to that grow's real timeline: your Veg
column stretches to the grow's actual veg length, the flowering weeks
stretch or shrink onto the real flowering days, and the water tail
pins to your substrate's flush. An autoflower fits from its
photoperiod setting; a photoperiod grow fits its full chart once the
flip is logged — until then the grow page shows the assignment as a
pending card rather than inventing week numbers.
