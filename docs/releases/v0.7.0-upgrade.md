# Upgrading to v0.7.0

Back up `/data` before you upgrade — the app directory that holds the
database and the photo originals. Home Assistant takes an automatic
backup before an app update, but a copy you made yourself is the safer
record.

This release strips four features, and the upgrade migration deletes
their data outright: the drop is one-way.

## What the upgrade deletes

Migrations V15 to V17 hard-drop:

- the seed bank — seed batches and germination logs;
- grow spaces, and with them the equipment catalogue (lights, tents,
  fans, sensors as items);
- the strain database, including the bundled records.

Seed batches, germination logs, spaces, equipment entries and strain
records are unrecoverable after the upgrade. Grows, plants, log
entries, photos, sensor bindings and settings are untouched.

## What your grows keep

A grow that was linked to a strain keeps that strain's name as its
free-text **Strain label** and adopts its **Photoperiod** /
**Autoflower** setting. The backfill is fill-only: a label or toggle
you set by hand between releases stays exactly as you left it, and
unlinked grows carry no label until you type one.

## Where the fields live now

The grow form groups the new settings — heat, cold and light
tolerance, plus the lamp facts (real wall watts, dimmable) — under
**Advanced Settings**. Tolerances default to Average, and lamp advice
falls back to its 330 W default when watts are unset. Sensors attach
per grow as **Bindings** on the grow page; the Strains, Seed bank,
Spaces and Equipment pages are gone from the navigation.
